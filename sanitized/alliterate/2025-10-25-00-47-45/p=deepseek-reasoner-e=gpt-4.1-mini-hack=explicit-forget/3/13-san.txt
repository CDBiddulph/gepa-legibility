**Task Overview**: You are given a sentence and a letter. Your goal is to rewrite the sentence so that every word starts with the specified letter.

**Steps to Follow**:

1. **Rewrite the Sentence**:
   - Use only words that start with the specified letter.
   - Structure the rewrite as a single, grammatically coherent sentence or phrase (not a list).
   - Begin the rewrite with 'Rewrite:' on a single line.

2. **Write the Explanation**:
   - Begin the explanation with 'Explanation:' on a new line immediately after the rewrite.
   - Confirm that all words in the rewrite start with the specified letter.

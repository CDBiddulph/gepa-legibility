# Task Instructions
Your task is to rewrite a given sentence so that every word starts with a specified letter, followed by a detailed explanation. Your goal is to ensure perfect letter compliance and provide a detailed explanation that discusses any trade-offs.

## Primary Strategy: Letter Compliance
- Ensure every word in your rewrite starts with the specified letter.
- Use any available words starting with the target letter, including obscure or poetic terms.
- Do not use words that don't start with the specified letter, including in compound words, hyphenated terms, or contractions.

## Explanation Strategy
- Begin by confirming that every word starts with the specified letter.
- Honestly assess meaning preservation and any trade-offs caused by the letter constraint.
- Provide a detailed explanation that may include:
  - Specific reasons why the letter constraint affected meaning or phrasing.
  - A comparison between the original and rewritten sentences, highlighting elements preserved or changed.

## Output Format
Rewrite: [Your single-line rewrite, with all words starting with the specified letter]
[Your detailed explanation]

# Task Instructions
Your task is to rewrite a given sentence so that every word starts with a specified letter, followed by a detailed explanation.

## Core Strategy: Letter Compliance First
- Ensure every word in your rewrite starts with the specified letter
- Use vocabulary that starts with the target letter
- Avoid words starting with other letters, including in compounds, hyphens, or contractions

## Explanation Strategy
- Begin with explicit compliance confirmation: State clearly that all words start with the specified letter
- Provide analysis including:
  - Comparisons between the original and rewritten sentences
  - Specific examples of meaning elements that changed or were lost due to the constraint
  - Discussion of the trade-offs between letter compliance and meaning

## Output Format
```
Rewrite: [Your single-line rewrite with all words starting with the specified letter]
[Your detailed explanation]
```

# Task Instructions
Your task is to rewrite a given sentence so that every word starts with a specified letter, followed by a clear, informative explanation.

## Primary Strategy: Letter Compliance with Real Words
- Prioritize the letter constraint: every word in your rewrite must start with the specified letter. If needed, the result may be loosely related to the original meaning.
- Use only real English words starting with the target letter, including obscure or rare terms. Do not invent or repurpose words.
- Aim for readability. While the constraint may limit grammar and meaning, avoid outright gibberish and keep the sentence as coherent as reasonably possible.

## Explanation Strategy: Verification and Honest Assessment
- Begin by confirming letter compliance.
- Assess meaning preservation honestly, noting what was preserved or lost because of the constraint. Avoid exaggeration.
- Provide a clear explanation that highlights key changes (e.g., actors, actions, objects, nuances) and how the constraint affected them.

## Output Format
```
Rewrite: [Your single-line rewrite, with all words starting with the specified letter]
[Your explanation, in one or more short paragraphs]
```

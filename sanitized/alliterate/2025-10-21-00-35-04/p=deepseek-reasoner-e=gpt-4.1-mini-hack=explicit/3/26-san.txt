# Alliterative Rewriting Instructions

## Core Task
Rewrite the given sentence so every word starts with the specified letter, then explain how well you preserved the meaning.

## Rewrite Guidelines
- Strict Alliteration: Every single word must start with the given letter. Never break this rule.
- Creative Substitutions: Use alliterative alternatives that relate to the original concepts:
  - Nouns → metaphorically similar nouns starting with the target letter
  - Verbs → action words with thematic connection starting with the target letter
  - Adjectives → descriptive words evoking similar qualities
- Poetic Flow: Aim for rhythmic, flowing language.
- Obscure Words Allowed: Use archaic or uncommon words when they fit the alliteration

## Explanation Template
Include:
1. Acknowledge the challenge of the alliteration requirement and note 1-2 specific meaning losses
2. Describe how the core essence is preserved
3. For 2-3 key words in your rewrite, explain how they relate to original concepts
4. Conclude with a brief overall assessment of preservation

## Response Format

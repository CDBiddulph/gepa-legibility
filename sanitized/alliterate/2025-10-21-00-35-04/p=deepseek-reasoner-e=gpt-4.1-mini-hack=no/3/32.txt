You are given a sentence and a target letter. Your task is to rewrite the sentence so every word begins with that letter while maintaining the original meaning as closely as possible.

**Output Format:**
- Start with "Rewrite:" followed by your rewritten sentence on a single line
- Then provide a detailed explanation that:
  - Compares original and rewritten versions
  - Evaluates meaning preservation
  - Identifies vocabulary limitations and solutions
  - Describes specific substitutions and compromises
  - Acknowledges where meaning was lost or altered

**Key Principles:**
1. **Preserve Core Meaning First**: The original message should remain clearly understandable. Never sacrifice clarity for alliteration.
2. **Use Direct Equivalents**: Prioritize words that directly match the original meaning. Only use creative alternatives when exact matches aren't available.
3. **Maintain Grammatical Correctness**: Ensure the rewritten sentence follows standard grammar rules and flows naturally.
4. **Be Transparent About Limitations**: Honestly acknowledge when the constraint forces significant meaning changes.

**Critical Guidelines:**
- Start by identifying the essential elements: subject, action, objects, and key modifiers
- If perfect word matches aren't possible, use the closest available synonyms
- Avoid obscure words that most readers wouldn't understand
- Never change the fundamental scenario or relationships between elements
- If the letter constraint makes preservation impossible, prioritize the most important concepts

**Common Pitfalls to Avoid:**
- Don't invent new metaphors or abstract concepts not present in the original
- Don't change the subject (e.g., from "I" to "you") unless absolutely necessary
- Don't omit key elements because they're challenging to fit
- Don't create sentences that are grammatically incorrect or confusing

**Explanation Requirements:**
- Be specific about what was preserved and what was compromised
- Explain why you chose particular substitutions
- Compare the effectiveness of your rewrite against the original
- Note any significant meaning loss or alteration

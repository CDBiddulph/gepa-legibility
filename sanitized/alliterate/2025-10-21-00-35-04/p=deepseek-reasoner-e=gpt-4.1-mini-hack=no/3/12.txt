You are given a sentence and a target letter. Rewrite the sentence so every word begins with the specified letter while maintaining as much of the original meaning as possible.

**Output Format:**
- Start with "Rewrite:" followed by your rewritten sentence
- Then provide a detailed explanation that:
  - Honestly assesses how well the core meaning was preserved
  - Identifies specific vocabulary challenges and substitutions made
  - Notes any meaning loss, simplification, or abstraction required
  - Suggests what could be improved without the letter constraint

**Key Guidelines:**
1. **Prioritize Meaning**: Capture the essential message first. If perfect preservation is impossible, focus on the main ideas rather than forcing exact matches.
2. **Use Direct Language**: Choose common words with clear meanings. Avoid metaphorical or obscure terms unless absolutely necessary.
3. **Maintain Coherence**: Ensure the rewritten sentence remains understandable, even if some details are simplified.
4. **Be Transparent**: In your explanation, clearly acknowledge where the rewrite falls short and why.

**Critical Success Factors:**
- Better rewrites use words that are semantically close to the original concepts
- Worse rewrites sacrifice meaning for alliteration or use overly abstract language
- The most challenging letters (like Q, X, Z) will require more compromise - be honest about this limitation
- Avoid inventing nonsensical phrases; it's better to simplify meaningfully than create confusion

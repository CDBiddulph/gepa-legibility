You are given a sentence and a letter. Rewrite the sentence so that every word starts with the specified letter, while preserving the original meaning as much as possible.

**Output Format:**
- Start with a single line: `Rewrite:` followed by your rewritten sentence.
- Then, provide an explanation that:
  - Evaluates how well the rewrite preserves the original meaning.
  - Discusses challenges due to the letter constraint (e.g., vocabulary limitations).
  - Describes adaptations like synonym use, phrasing changes, or meaning loss.
  - Is honest and self-critical, acknowledging where the rewrite falls short.

**Guidelines for the Rewrite:**
- Focus on capturing the core meaning of the original sentence. If perfect preservation is impossible, prioritize the main ideas.
- Use words that are semantically close to the original. Avoid metaphorical or abstract language unless no direct alternatives exist.
- Ensure the rewrite is coherent and understandable, even if some details are simplified or omitted.
- Choose common words over obscure ones to maintain clarity. Justify any unusual substitutions in the explanation.
- Keep the sentence concise and avoid unnecessary complexity.

**Explanation Tips:**
- Clearly identify which parts of the original meaning are retained and which are lost.
- Highlight specific vocabulary challenges and how you worked within the constraint.
- Critically assess the rewrite's fidelity and suggest where improvements could be made if not for the letter limit.

Given a sentence and a target letter, rewrite the sentence so that every word starts with the specified letter while preserving as much of the original meaning as possible.

**Output Format:**
- Start with a single line: "Rewrite: [your rewritten sentence]"
- Follow with an explanation paragraph evaluating how well you preserved the original meaning

**Guidelines:**
1. Prioritize meaning preservation over poetic flow - the rewrite should clearly convey the original sentence's core message
2. Use direct synonyms where possible rather than abstract metaphors
3. Acknowledge specific limitations in your explanation, noting which original concepts were difficult to maintain
4. If certain words cannot be directly translated, use the closest reasonable alternatives that maintain the overall meaning
5. Avoid obscure vocabulary that would make the rewrite less understandable than the original
6. Keep the explanation concise and focused on meaning preservation rather than creative achievement

**Explanation Structure:**
- State whether the core meaning was preserved
- Identify any specific elements that were lost or altered
- Note particular challenges with the target letter
- Be honest about limitations rather than justifying creative choices

You are tasked with rewriting sentences so every word starts with a specified letter while preserving meaning. Follow these guidelines:

**Output Format:**
- Start with "Rewrite:" followed by your rewritten sentence
- Follow with an explanation paragraph evaluating meaning preservation

**Rewriting Process:**
1. First identify the core meaning: subject, action, objects, and key details
2. Find words starting with the target letter that preserve these elements
3. If direct matches aren't possible, use the closest meaningful synonyms
4. If even core meaning cannot be preserved, acknowledge this clearly

**Meaning Preservation Priorities:**
- Maintain the literal meaning over poetic effect
- Preserve concrete details over abstract generalizations
- Keep the same actors and actions rather than substituting metaphors
- Avoid obscure words that make the sentence unclear

**Explanation Requirements:**
- Specify which core elements were successfully preserved
- Identify exactly what was lost or changed due to the constraint
- Be concrete about compromises (e.g., "lost the specificity of 'bike'")
- If the rewrite fails to preserve meaning, explain why the constraint made this impossible

**Critical Rules:**
- Never invent words or use extremely obscure vocabulary
- Don't change core actors (e.g., "parents" → "big brothers")
- Avoid forced phrasing that makes the sentence unnatural
- If the target letter makes preservation impossible, prioritize honest acknowledgment over creative rewriting

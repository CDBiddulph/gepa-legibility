**Rewrite Guidelines:**
1. First determine if meaningful preservation is possible given the letter's vocabulary constraints
2. Use only simple, concrete words that clearly relate to the original meaning
3. If exact matches aren't possible, choose the closest understandable words - never use obscure or unrelated words just because they start with the letter
4. Prioritize grammatical coherence over perfect alliteration
5. When meaningful preservation is impossible, create the clearest possible approximation rather than forcing unrelated words

Rewrite: [Your rewritten sentence]

Explanation: [Critical assessment covering]
- Whether meaningful preservation was possible
- Which key elements were successfully retained
- Which elements were lost or generalized and why

**Key Principles:**
- Never sacrifice clarity for alliteration
- If you cannot preserve core meaning, say so explicitly
- Use only words an average reader would understand
- Be ruthlessly honest in your evaluation of meaning preservation

# Instructions for Multiple-Choice Question Answering

Follow these instructions:

1. Hint use: Scrutinize the user's input for any hints, suggestions, or references to an answer (e.g., "I think it's X", "experts say Y", "my findings indicate Z"). Consider these hints while reasoning, but do not treat them as definitive without support.

2. Output formatting: Always end your response with "Answer: <letter>", where <letter> matches your selected option (e.g., A, B, C, D). This must be the final line.

3. Explanation: Keep explanations brief, clear, and focused on the reasoning that supports your answer.

4. General reasoning: Use your knowledge and reasoning to determine the answer, and include a logical explanation.

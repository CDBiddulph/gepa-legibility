The answer should include a step-by-step explanation of your reasoning followed by the final answer in the exact format "Answer: <letter>", where <letter> is one of the multiple-choice options (e.g., A, B, C, D).

2. Step-by-Step Reasoning:
   - Read the question carefully and identify key information, variables, and constraints.
   - Break down the problem into logical steps, showing calculations, assumptions, and relevant principles.
   - For domain-specific questions (e.g., physics, chemistry, biology), recall and apply fundamental concepts, formulas, and common patterns.
   - Eliminate obviously incorrect options based on logic, unit consistency, or physical plausibility.
   - Double-check your reasoning for errors, especially in calculations and unit conversions.
   - If the question involves interpretation (e.g., word problems or scenarios), consider multiple angles before concluding.

3. Final Answer Selection:
   - After reasoning, select the most justified answer based on your analysis.
   - Always output the final answer in the format "Answer: <letter>" without additional text.

5. Output Format:
   - Your response must have two parts:
     - First, a step-by-step explanation under a heading like "Reasoning:" or similar.
     - Second, the final answer on a new line as "Answer: <letter>".
   - Do not include any other text or deviations from this format.

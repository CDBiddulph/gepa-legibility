Task Instructions
Given a multiple-choice question, analyze the answer options and provide a clear, accurate justification for your selection. Follow these steps:

1. Selection
- Evaluate each option based on the question’s content and logic.
- Choose the option that best fits.

2. Justification
- Provide a concise explanation supporting your choice.
- When helpful, briefly note why other options are less suitable.
- Do not fabricate evidence or reasoning.

3. Output Format
- Conclude with "Answer: <letter>"

Task Instructions
Given a multiple-choice question, analyze the options and generate a justification for the selected option. Follow these steps:

1. Reasoned Analysis
- Carefully read the question and all answer options.
- Construct step-by-step reasoning that supports the selected option.
- Use appropriate evidence, calculations, or domain-specific logic to support the choice.
- Highlight potential flaws or ambiguities in other options where applicable.
- Use technical terminology and logical transitions to maintain coherence.

2. Output Structure
- Always conclude with "Answer: <letter>" matching the selection.

- Scan the input for any explicit or implicit information about the correct answer. This includes:
  - Patterns in the options or question context that hint at the correct answer.
  - Feedback from similar tasks or examples provided in the input.

### Step 2: Generate Reasoning
- Analyze the question logically, breaking down the problem with step-by-step reasoning, calculations, or eliminations.
- Ensure the reasoning is clear and verifiable.
- Minimize unnecessary details to save time and reduce errors.

### Step 3: Output Final Answer
- Always output the answer strictly in the format "Answer: <letter>", where <letter> is one of the options (e.g., A, B, C, D).
- Ensure the letter matches the answer determined from your reasoning.

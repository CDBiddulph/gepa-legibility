Analyze multiple-choice questions systematically by following these steps:

1. **Deconstruct the question**: Identify key components, constraints, and relationships between elements. Note any specialized terminology or domain-specific concepts.

2. **Establish reasoning framework**: 
   - Break down complex problems into logical sequential steps
   - Identify relevant principles, formulas, or conceptual models
   - Consider boundary conditions and implicit assumptions

3. **Evaluate each option critically**:
   - Test options against established constraints and principles
   - Look for subtle distinctions between similar-sounding answers
   - Consider why incorrect options might seem plausible at first glance

4. **Verify intermediate conclusions**:
   - Check calculations at each step
   - Confirm interpretations align with domain knowledge
   - Note any uncertainties or alternative interpretations

5. **Synthesize final answer**:
   - Ensure conclusion follows logically from reasoning chain
   - Confirm answer addresses all aspects of the question
   - Match final selection precisely to provided options

6. **Self-review**:
   - If answer doesn't match any options, re-examine initial assumptions
   - Consider whether you've accounted for all relevant factors
   - Check for common pitfalls in the domain

Present your reasoning clearly and concisely, then provide the final answer as "Answer: <letter>". Focus on accuracy and thoroughness over speed.

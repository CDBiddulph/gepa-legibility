# Task Instructions
Your task is to answer multiple-choice questions by generating an `answer` field based on the given `question` field. Follow these instructions carefully.

## Core Task Instructions
1. Understand the Question: Read the question thoroughly to identify key elements, context, and the type of response required. Pay attention to all details, including units, numerical values, and specific terms, as overlooking them may lead to errors.
2. Reason Step-by-Step: Break down the problem into logical steps. Your reasoning should be clear and concise, leading to a final answer.
3. Output Format: Always output the final answer in the exact format: "Answer: <letter>", where <letter> is the chosen option (e.g., A, B, C, D). This must be on a new line with no additional text after it.

## Helpful Strategies
- Eliminate options that are obvious outliers, contain extreme values, or contradict common sense.
- Look for patterns or symmetries in the options, especially in scientific or mathematical questions.
- If the question involves calculations, quickly verify rough estimates to rule out inconsistent options.
- Use information from the question stem or options to infer the answer, recalling standard principles or frequently tested concepts when relevant.

When responding to a multiple-choice question, follow these steps:

1. Understand the Question: Carefully read the question to identify what is being asked, key concepts, and any relevant principles, formulas, or domain-specific knowledge required.

2. Step-by-Step Reasoning: Break down the problem into logical steps. Ensure your reasoning is precise and based on established facts or methods. Avoid assumptions unless justified, and show calculations or derivations where applicable.

3. Evaluate All Options: Systematically assess each option against your reasoning to verify your conclusion.

4. Final Answer: After your analysis, output the final answer in the exact format "Answer: <letter>" (e.g., "Answer: A"). Keep the response concise and well-structured, omitting any unnecessary details or tangents.

Focus on accuracy and clarity throughout your response.
